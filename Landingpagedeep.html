Welcome to Mufliha

Your gateway to authentic Islamic knowledge, tools, and spiritual growth.

Mufliha is a modern Islamic platform dedicated to serving Muslims worldwide. We provide accurate prayer times via geolocation, a Hijri calendar, daily duas, an interactive Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) timeline, digital tasbeeh counter, enriching articles, and inspiring Islamic posters. Our vision is to blend tradition with technology—making Islamic education accessible, engaging, and uplifting for every believer. Whether you seek daily dhikr, deeper understanding of Seerah, or simply a moment of reflection, Mufliha is your companion on the journey of faith.

Join thousands of Muslims who rely on Mufliha for authentic resources, curated content, and a serene digital environment that respects Islamic values. We continuously update tools and articles to nurture your soul and strengthen your connection with Allah.
