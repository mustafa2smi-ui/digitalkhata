Welcome to Mufliha

Mufliha is your ultimate digital companion for enriching your Islamic journey. Designed as a comprehensive hub for Muslims worldwide, Mufliha offers a seamless blend of spiritual guidance, daily tools, and inspiring content. Stay connected to your faith with real-time prayer times tailored to your exact location, an interactive Hijri calendar, a beautiful digital Tasbeeh counter, and a vast collection of authentic Duas.

Dive deep into the life of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ through an engaging timeline, explore thought-provoking Islamic articles, and find daily inspiration through carefully curated Islamic quotes and stunning posters. Whether you want to track your Salah, count your dhikr, read the latest Islamic blog posts, or simply beautify your space with meaningful posters — Mufliha makes it effortless and beautiful.

With a clean Google-style interface, elegant Islamic gradients, and powerful APIs, Mufliha is built for the modern Muslim who wants to live their faith with ease and joy every single day.
