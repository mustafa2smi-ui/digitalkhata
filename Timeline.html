571 AD

The Year of Elephant / Birth

Paigambar Mohammad (PBUH) ki wiladat Makkah shareef me hui. Is saal ko 'Aam al-Fil' bhi kaha jata hai kyunki isi saal Abrahah ne Kaaba par hathi le kar hamla kiya tha.

610 AD

First Revelation (Wahi)

Jabal-e-Noor ke Ghaar-e-Hira me Hazrat Jibraeel (A.S) nazil hue aur Surah Al-Alaq ki shuruati aayat nazil huin. Ye Nubuwwat ka aaghaz tha.

622 AD

Hijrat to Madinah

Makkah ke kuffar ke zulm se tang aakar Allah ke hukm se Aap (PBUH) ne apne sahaba ke sath Madinah (Yathrib) ki taraf hijrat ki.
