📅 Date: 1/1/571 AD

Birth of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)

Paigambar Muhammad (PBUH) ki wiladat Makkah sheher mein hui. Ye saal Arab mein 'Aam al-Fil' ke naam se jana jata hai. Aapki paidaish se pehle hi aapke walid Hazrat Abdullah (R.A) ka inteqal ho chuka tha. Aapki ammi Hazrat Amina (R.A) ne aapka naam Muhammad rakha... (Yaha apni puri detail likhein).
