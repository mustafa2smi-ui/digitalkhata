Digital Khata vs Paper Register: Dukandaron ke liye kaunsa behtar hai?

Published on Jan 7, 2026 | By DigitalKhata Team

Aaj ke digital yug mein, chhote business ko badhane ke liye purane tareekon ko badalna zaroori hai. Dukandar barso se kagaz ke register par udhari aur hisab likhte aa rahe hain, lekin kya ye aaj bhi sahi hai?

Paper Register ki Kamiyan

Manual register use karne se business mein kai tarah ki mushkilein aati hain:

  • Gum hone ka darr: Register phat sakta hai, pani se kharab ho sakta hai ya kahin kho sakta hai.
  • Waqt ki barbadi: Purana hisab dhundhne mein ghanton lagte hain jab grahak samne khada ho.
  • Manual Calculation Errors: Insaan se galti ho sakti hai, jo business mein nuksan ka karan banti hai.

Digital Khata ke Fayde

Hamara Digital Khata Tool istemal karne se aapka business smart banta hai:

  • Turant Calculation: Items add karte hi automatic total ho jata hai.
  • Transparency: Grahak ko har item ka clear hisab dikhta hai jisse bharosa badhta hai.
  • Easy Access: Kahin bhi, kabhi bhi apne mobile par pichla baki check karein.
